Frequently asked questions

What is BMI?

Body mass index is your weight divided by your height squared. It gives one number that places you in a broad category, from underweight to obese.

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults, 18.5 to 24.9 is classed as healthy. It is a broad guide, not a personal diagnosis.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

Not always. Muscle weighs more than fat, so a muscular person can score as overweight while being lean.

Does BMI work for children?

Children are judged against age and sex percentiles, not the adult cut-offs, so use a paediatric chart for children.

What is waist-to-height ratio?

Your waist divided by your height. Keeping it below 0.5 is a widely used rule of thumb for health.

How often should I check my BMI?

Once a month is plenty. Weigh yourself the same way each time and watch the trend rather than one reading.

Is the calculator free?

Yes, every tool on this site is free and runs in your browser with no sign-up.

Do you store my weight?

No. All calculations happen in your browser and nothing is sent or stored.
